# Data Subject Access Request (DSAR): Worked Case Study

A single DSAR walked through its full lifecycle at a financial-services firm: intake, identity verification, locating data across systems, applying exemptions, preparing the response, and keeping the audit trail.

This is an illustrative exercise using a fictional requester and a representative financial-services context, no real personal data involved. Modelled under EU GDPR in a Finnish context, referencing the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto) as the supervisory authority.

Access requests are one of the most common tasks in privacy operations, and the deadline is rarely the hard part. The real difficulty is finding data scattered across systems, applying exemptions correctly, and keeping a record that would hold up if challenged. This case study works through that end to end on a single realistic request.

## The scenario

A customer of a financial-services firm requests access to all personal data the firm holds about them. Under GDPR, the firm has one month to respond, must provide the data along with the required accompanying information, and must do so without exposing anyone else's personal data or protected material. The data itself is spread across banking, CRM, marketing, and call-centre systems.

## What this covers

A complete DSAR handling record: the intake log, identity verification step, a data-location map across systems, an exemptions analysis, a drafted response package, and the audit trail.

| Step | What happens | GDPR anchor |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| 1 | Intake and logging | Art. 12 |
| 2 | Identity verification | Art. 12 |
| 3 | Locating the data | Art. 15 |
| 4 | Exemptions analysis | Art. 15(4), rights of others |
| 5 | Response package | Art. 15 |
| 6 | Audit trail and close | Art. 5(2) |

## Why it matters

A repeatable, defensible DSAR process protects a firm from complaints and regulatory findings, and turns what's often an ad-hoc scramble into a controlled process with an evidence trail behind it.
